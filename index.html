What We Ship

Products

AI software we build for regulated industries — described only by what each product does today.

Modular Compliance

NZ-built compliance register and workflow tool. Maps legislation into frameworks, controls and tasks, records who completed what and when, and stores supporting evidence — people do the compliance work, the tool keeps the record.

modularcompliance.com

BossBoard

Health & safety compliance for NZ tradespeople. Manage site diaries, incident reports, SWMS, and subcontractor records — all from mobile or desktop.

Free during beta · Paid tiers from NZ$4.99/week at launch bossboard.instilligent.com

Mastering MOSS

Maritime Operator Safety System for NZ commercial maritime — MTOP/MTOC document builder, vessel and crew registry, compliance checklists, maintenance and safety-equipment tracking. Aligned with NZ Maritime Rules and the Maritime Transport Act.

From NZ$29/month · tiered by fleet size masteringmoss.co.nz

CodeHumanist

Read-only codebase comprehension for AI-generated code — architecture, data flows and use cases for stakeholders who don't read code. Early access waitlist.

codehumanist.com — Waitlist

ProofOnce

A planned document and identity verification product — verify once, reuse the proof across consumers. Pre-launch.

proofonce.com — Coming Soon
